DESIGNED WITH CLIENT WORK IN MIND

Your account metadata stays with you.

SuiteCraft works through an already authenticated NetSuite browser tab. Catalogue snapshots, saved reference values, Favorites, Change Plans and notes are stored locally and are not sent to a SuiteCraft backend. Small interface preferences may sync through your browser profile.
