DESIGNED WITH CLIENT WORK IN MIND

Your account metadata stays with you.

SuiteCraft works through an already authenticated NetSuite browser tab. Catalogue snapshots, favorites and notes are stored locally in the browser profile and are not sent to a SuiteCraft backend.

0.1.0-dev Build 0.1.0.2

September 9, 2026
New
  • Share selected snapshot versions safely between authorized SuiteCraft users.
  • Inspect scripts and applicable workflows for the current NetSuite record.
  • Refresh selected catalogue objects without repeating a full account synchronization.
Improved
  • Deeper Record Object inspection with sublist discovery, session pins and clearer field actions.
  • Resume interrupted synchronization while retaining verified workflow details.
  • More practical snapshot comparison with deeper SDF inspection.

Shared snapshot packages retain their source account, role and timestamps, while excluding settings, Favorites and private notes.
