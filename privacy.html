SUITECRAFT BROWSER EXTENSION

Privacy policy.

SuiteCraft is designed as a local-first toolkit. This policy explains what the extension accesses, where its data is stored and which actions remain under your control.

Effective: September 10, 2026

Who operates SuiteCraft

SuiteCraft is an independent browser extension developed and maintained by Damian Krolikowski. It is not affiliated with Oracle or NetSuite.

Information the extension accesses

SuiteCraft runs on NetSuite pages where you are already authenticated. Depending on the feature you choose, it may read the current page and technical NetSuite metadata, including record fields and sublists, customization identifiers and labels, custom records, lists, scripts, deployments, workflows and SDF definitions.

SuiteCraft does not ask for or intentionally collect your NetSuite password. It is not intended to collect customer transactions, credentials, financial information or other business content.

Local storage

Preferences, account-scoped metadata snapshots, SDF definitions, Favorites and notes are stored in your browser profile using browser storage and IndexedDB. SuiteCraft does not operate a cloud account or backend to receive this data.

Local data remains until you delete it in SuiteCraft, clear the extension's browser storage or uninstall the extension. You can export settings and Workspace data to local JSON or CSV files. Those files leave the browser only when you explicitly save or share them.

Sharing and selling

SuiteCraft does not sell local data, use it for advertising or transfer it to third parties. Snapshot sharing and backup export are explicit user actions; the user chooses the resulting file and its recipient.

Limited Use compliance

SuiteCraft's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. SuiteCraft uses data only to provide or improve its disclosed user-facing features. It does not use or transfer user data for personalized advertising, creditworthiness or lending purposes, and does not permit humans to read user data unless the user explicitly shares specific information for support, it is required for security, or it is required by law.

Analytics and external links

The SuiteCraft extension does not currently send product analytics. Links to the SuiteCraft website, Tally feedback form and Buy Me a Coffee page open only when you select them and are then governed by those services' own privacy policies.

The public SuiteCraft marketing website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate traffic and performance statistics. That analytics beacon is not bundled with the browser extension.

Browser permissions

  • Storage keeps settings and local Workspace data.
  • Active tab lets user-initiated actions work with the selected NetSuite page.
  • Access to https://*.netsuite.com/* lets SuiteCraft provide its tools on NetSuite pages and retrieve metadata when you request synchronization or inspection.

Security and responsible testing

No browser extension can eliminate every risk. Keep your browser and SuiteCraft current, share exported files only with authorized people, and do not include confidential NetSuite information in feedback reports.

Children

SuiteCraft is a professional tool and is not directed to children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when SuiteCraft's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions or deletion guidance, use the SuiteCraft feedback form.
